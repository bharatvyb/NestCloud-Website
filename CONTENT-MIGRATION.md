## Product-model normalization
There is one product, NestCloud Family. 512 GB costs ₹49,900; 1 TB costs ₹59,900; 1 TB + 1 TB Backup Plus costs ₹81,900. Backup Plus has 1 TB usable storage and a separate physical 1 TB backup SSD. Shared albums, remote access, personal accounts, Photos, Files, the dashboard and supported updates are common software features. No legacy edition account limits or feature gates were carried over.

## Availability and claims
Family Box Office is planned for OS v1.1, with the original site describing a free update. Storage expansion is planned for OS v1.2; up to 4 TB additional capacity remains subject to release and compatibility confirmation. Legacy target months, launch dates, first-50 reservation scarcity, preorder advances, unconfirmed delivery promises and edition-specific upgrade prices were not adopted as current offers on this development site.

Absolute privacy, permanent data protection, zero-loss and guaranteed-savings language was replaced with specific explanations of local storage, sharing controls, connectivity dependencies and off-device backup responsibilities. SSD ratings are separated from real network transfer performance.

## Pages and interactions
Nine exported routes: homepage, Family, OS, Why NestCloud, How It Works, Backup Plus, Support, Order, and Policies. Native navigation works with direct visits and refreshes. Every route has unique title, description, Open Graph metadata and canonical URL. The sitemap omits the noindexed order preparation page.

The storage configurator sends its selected configuration to the order page. Order changes update the summary, product description and shareable configuration query. No payments, orders, reservations or personal information are collected. Real policy documents link to the official site rather than inventing new legal text.

Support includes twenty searchable FAQs, topic filters, a no-results state and an issue-specific troubleshooting selector. Support actions open the verified official support route and do not automatically send messages.

## Remaining launch dependencies
Approved Razorpay order flow and credentials; current inventory and availability; confirmed tax and shipping treatment; final applicable purchase terms. Payments remain unavailable until these are supplied and approved.
